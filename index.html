Why Selective Clearing?

When most people think land clearing, they think dozers. And for large scale commercial clearing, that makes sense. But for rural property owners who want their land opened up thoughtfully — not scraped flat — there's a better way.

Selective clearing preserves what's worth keeping. Mature hardwood, established tree lines, natural drainage, the character of the land you bought in the first place. A dozer doesn't make those distinctions. A skilled operator with a chainsaw and a tractor does.

ACB Services specializes in the clearing work that heavy equipment can't do well — or shouldn't. Brush removal and alder clearing that leaves mature trees standing. Driveway and trail corridors cut through mixed hardwood without destroying the canopy. Riparian clearing near creeks and wetlands where equipment access is limited or regulated. Woodlot thinning that improves the stand rather than eliminating it.

The result is land that looks intentional. Open where it needs to be, wooded where it should stay, ready for whatever comes next — a cabin site, a garden, a sugar bush, a view.

If you're not sure what your property needs, we're happy to walk it with you and give you an honest assessment. No obligation, no upsell. Just a straight answer about what makes sense for your land.
